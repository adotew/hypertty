# App icons

Icons for programs running in a terminal, shown in the tab list.

- All but `openai.svg` come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org)
  16.36.0, released under CC0 1.0.
- `openai.svg` (used for Codex) comes from
  [homarr-labs/dashboard-icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons),
  licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

The logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the
program that is running.
